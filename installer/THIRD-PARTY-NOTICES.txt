Third-party software bundled in the "Full" installer (folder "offline")
=====================================================================

These files are only used by Chain Proxy's first-run setup (Run.bat) and by the Lantern option, when they
are not already installed. Each runs as a SEPARATE program; Chain Proxy itself (MIT) is not linked to them.

sing-box 1.14.4 (sing-box-1.14.4-windows-amd64.zip) - unmodified
  Source:  https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box/releases  (source code: https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box)
  License: GNU GPL v3 or later with additional terms, https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box/blob/main/LICENSE

Lantern engine "chainlantern" (chainlantern-72fe1e9-windows-amd64.zip)
  Lantern's open-source client backend, https://github.com/getlantern/radiance at commit
  72fe1e906ee530a9784e9c1337b26d1a198fe73f, built headless with the `novpn` tag (local SOCKS5 inbound, no TUN) plus a
  small launcher, dev/lantern/chainlantern/main.go in https://github.com/mhasgari/chainproxy-win.
  License: GNU GPL v3 or later (radiance, sing-box-minimal, and the other Lantern libraries it links; the zip carries
  the LICENSE text and SOURCE.txt). Corresponding source: the two repositories above, plus the Go modules listed in
  radiance's go.mod / go.sum. Rebuild with `python3 dev/build_lantern.py` (see its docstring).
